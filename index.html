About me

I didn’t begin in AI. I chose to move into it.

My earlier academic path was in Urban Planning and Urban Design. Artificial Intelligence is my current field of study and the direction I deliberately chose for my next chapter.

The transition

Changing fields without leaving the way I think behind.

Studying cities trained me to think about complex systems, people, evidence, spatial relationships, and decisions. When I moved into AI, I did not arrive with a computer-science background; I arrived as a beginner willing to rebuild my foundations carefully.

That transition now shapes how I approach AI: I want to understand the technical side deeply, but I also care about whether a system is useful, interpretable, and connected to a real human need.
