About me

A deliberate change of direction — not a straight line.

I did not arrive in AI from a computer-science background. I came from years of studying cities, people, evidence, and complex systems — and chose to begin again in a new technical field.

The transition

Different field. Same instinct: understand the system before trying to improve it.

Urban Planning taught me to see systems. Urban Design taught me to look closely at the people inside those systems. Both trained me to work with evidence, competing constraints, and problems that rarely have one simple answer.

AI is a new technical chapter for me. I am rebuilding the foundations deliberately — from Python and machine learning to NLP, computer vision, and agentic systems — while bringing with me the research discipline, systems thinking, and human-centered perspective developed in my earlier work.

Around-the-Island Bike Tour in Taiwan

How do you explore Taiwan by bicycle in only six days—without turning the trip into an unsafe endurance challenge?

I designed a six-day group adventure for 10 participants using a hybrid strategy: scenic cycling sections combined with support-vehicle and train transfers. The plan balances route selection, safety, weather considerations, photography opportunities, cultural stops, and affordability within an estimated budget of USD 530 per person.
